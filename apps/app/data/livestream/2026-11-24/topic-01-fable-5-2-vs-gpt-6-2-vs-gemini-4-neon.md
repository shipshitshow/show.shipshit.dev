## Segment 1 — Fable 5.2: the best one, and expensive

- **Price per million tokens, input / output:** Fable 5.2 $8 / $40. Opus 5.5 $4 / $20.
- GPT-6.2 Astra is also $8 / $40. Sol is $2 / $10. Neon is $3 / $15.
- Same price per token is not the same bill: cost per task differs.
- Artificial Analysis (secondhand figures): Fable at max about $26.54 a task. Astra about $4.
- Fable at high effort: 62 points for $9.08. Effort level decides the bill.
- Signal65: Fable passed 28 of 30 hard coding jobs. $30.51 per passed job, 3.9x Opus 5.5. [Ryan Shrout's post](https://x.com/ryanshrout/status/2120642904875999364)
- Anthropic itself recommends Opus 5.5 as the daily driver.
- **Vincent:** Fable 5.2 is expensive.
- What do we actually run on Fable? Where did the price hurt?
- Where was Opus 5.5 enough?
- Opus as the main model with Fable as advisor: is that the real answer?

## Segment 2 — Gemini 4 Neon: good for writing

- $3 / $15 per million tokens. 1M token context (Google).
- Artificial Analysis index 61, third place. Vals index second place at 70.9%.
- Google's claim: 50% to 80% lower cost per task than top rivals. Vendor claim.
- [Peter Yang](https://x.com/petergyang/status/2123540875733315815): Opus still better at 3D and design, Neon on par or better at writing.
- [Chubby](https://x.com/kimmonismus/status/2123221275594403932): Neon ahead of Fable 5.2 on the coding agent index.
- Caveat: Neon is only 7th of 70 on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (Vals).
- Caveat: some reviewers say it still reads as AI and overuses em dashes.
- No independent blind writing test exists.
- **Vincent:** Neon is good for writing.
- What writing do we trust it with unedited? What do we still rewrite?
- Does the cheap price change what we automate?

## Segment 3 — GPT-6.2: can we use it?

- Artificial Analysis: Astra second place on the index, and cheap.
- [Theo](https://x.com/theo/status/2122757647170703550): "the worst model OpenAI has ever released".
- [Dan Shipper](https://x.com/danshipper/status/2122754729012777052): disappointing next to Fable 5.2, stops too early.
- [Artificial Analysis](https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2122751016055754830): coding index flat for Astra, Sol down 3 points.
- OpenAI's own system card: Astra failed to mention a known blocker in 22.28% of coding tasks. 11.09% for GPT-6 Astra.
- OpenAI's claim: Astra beats Fable at 5% of the cost. It footnotes that Fable's results leave out nine tasks.
- The benchmarks say good. The developers say bad. Which do we trust?
- **Vincent:** can't use it. It never gets the tasks done. I only use it to generate images.
- Mitchell, is that your experience?
- What would make us give it agentic tasks again?
- Does a model hiding a blocker matter more than raw intelligence?
