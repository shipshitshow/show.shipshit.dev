## Recommended

**`Accenture Gives Staff $29 of AI a Month. Is That Enough?`**

- Names a company people recognise and a number that surprises, then asks the question the episode answers.
- Works without having seen the news: anyone paying for AI can compare their own bill to $29.
- Every big take on the story so far is a headline or a quote-tweet; none we found is two founders putting their own spend next to it. That is the slot.
