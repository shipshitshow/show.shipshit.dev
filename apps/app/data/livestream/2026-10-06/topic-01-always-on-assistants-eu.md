## Sources — Can a founder in Europe even get one?

### Question and opening cue

The viewer wants an AI PA. Opening cue: three launched, and from Europe we can use one of them properly.

### Evidence to open

- **Meta Muse:** launched 8 Sep in the US, then Canada (18 Sep reported). It is not available anywhere in the EU, with no date announced. The reported reasons are the DMA (consent to combine Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and Messenger data) plus GDPR and the AI Act. The DMA reason is press attribution, not a Meta statement. [Trending Topics: Europe has to wait for Dots, Muse and Siri AI](https://www.trendingtopics.eu/dots-muse-siri-ai-europe/) · [Muse and the EU: no launch date, no DPA](https://technspire.com/en/blog/muse-and-the-eu-no-launch-date-no-dpa-and-your-options)
- **OpenAI dots:** Pro users in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK are excluded for now. OpenAI says Business Premium gets dots "across all supported ChatGPT regions", EU included. OpenAI has given no reason. [dots in Europe (Mad Robot)](https://madrobot.blog/2026/09/29/chatgpt-dots-uk-europe-release-date/) · [dots pricing and access (MindStudio)](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/chatgpt-dots-pricing-access)
- **The EU workaround is a business plan.** Business Premium is reported at $125 per seat/month (or $100 billed annually). A workspace needs at least two seats, so the cheapest route is roughly $120–150/month for one dot. Secondary source; check OpenAI's pricing page before quoting. [eesel: dots pricing](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/openai-dots-pricing)
- **Grok Bot:** no published regional restriction. Access follows the plan: Cursor Pro from $20, or SuperGrok from $30. Grok models opened to the EU API on 17 Jul. [Grok Bot availability by country](https://www.tryagentsonic.com/articles/grok-bot-availability-countries)
- Firsthand: we are in the EU. Vincent runs Grok Bot.app on the MacBook Pro.

## Sources — What each one is actually for

### Question and opening cue

Same promise ("always-on"), different jobs. Cue: Muse runs your errands, dots run your business software, Grok Bot runs your workflows. That framing is from the comparison sites; test it against the evidence.

### Evidence to open

- **Muse:** reads email, books travel and appointments, fills forms, negotiates, and pays with your card. Free tier (card required), Power $20/month (500M tokens/week), Maximum $100/month (3B/week). US, iOS/Android/web, 18+. [CNBC, 8 Sep](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/meta-personal-ai-agents-public-reckoning-privacy-safety.html) · [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/meta-launches-personal-ai-agent-muse-to-help-with-everyday-tasks)
- **dots:** each dot runs on GPT-6 Astra with its own cloud computer and browser, and works 24/7 toward a responsibility you give it. OpenAI's examples: monitor bug reports, prepare a budget cycle, move an app off a retiring API. One dot is included with Pro ($100) and Business Premium. [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/29/openai-dots-agent/) · [OpenAI launch video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uXspbC2srEQ)
- **Grok Bot:** a desktop agent with a terminal that can learn a task by watching you. Model not named by xAI. The SpaceX/Cursor team used it to build itself (our 1 Sep stream). [Comparison: AIMultiple](https://aimultiple.com/always-on-agents) · [Memeburn: three agents, three jobs](https://memeburn.com/openai-dots-vs-meta-muse-vs-grok-bot-three-always-on-agents-built-for-three-different-jobs/)
- Other people's tests (label them as not ours): [Nate Herk, dots vs Muse](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BvvfZKKz4Yo) · [Futurepedia, dots as a personal assistant](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_1Vn2WfpEY)
