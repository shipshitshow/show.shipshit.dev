## Sources — Useless regulation: Europe can't buy one

### Question and opening cue

Host take (Vincent): EU regulation is useless here. It didn't protect us from these products; it only decided which of us pay more for them, or wait. Cue: we are in the EU, and two of the three assistants aren't for sale to us.

### The case it is useless

- **Same product, higher price.** OpenAI blocks dots on personal Pro in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, but sells it to EU businesses on Business Premium (about $125/seat monthly, two-seat minimum, so roughly $120–150/month against $100 for Pro). Same agent, same data, different invoice. Prices are from snippets ([MindStudio](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/chatgpt-dots-pricing-access), [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/openai-dots-pricing)); check OpenAI's pricing page on air.
- **Grok Bot is legal here.** The same category of product, an agent with its own computer signing in to your tools, works for us in the EU. xAI lists no regional limits.
- **The EU missed its own deadline.** The AI Act's high-risk rules were due 2 Aug 2026. The Digital Omnibus, in force since 27 Jul 2026, pushed them to Dec 2027 (and Aug 2028 for AI in regulated products), because national authorities and the technical standards weren't ready.
