# Title options — 2026-10-06

**Working title (not locked):** `[LIVE] The AI PA Era: Are You Even Busy Enough For One?`

Scope: the always-on assistant wave (dots, Muse, Grok Bot) and whether it is sustainable, for the founder paying for one and for the lab selling it. Coding is the one job that fills 24 hours; take it out and most founders do not have enough for a 24/7 PA. The EU is a talking point inside the show, not the headline.

## Why this shape

- This week's biggest YouTube theme is always-on agents. OpenAI's dots launch video has 645K views, the stage-failure video 678K, Nate Herk's dots vs Muse test 354K. Views are from a yt-dlp scrape on 5 Oct.
- Lab-economics scepticism is the other big theme (Anthropic IPO videos at 532K and 137K). This title joins the two.
- The dots and Muse videos cover what the agents can do. None we found asks whether you have enough work for one. That question is ours.
- Our own best recent stream is a "How to use … Grok Bot" title (676 views). Small sample; older videos have had longer to collect views.

## Which to pick

- **Recommendation: option 1.** It names the era and asks the viewer the question the episode answers. Put dots, Muse and Grok Bot in the description and thumbnail brief for search.
- **Option 2** is the search-friendly version: all three product names plus the question. Pick it if discoverability matters more than the take.
- **Option 3 or 4** are the take-led versions, closest to our Anthropic opinion streams. Use them if both hosts land on "most founders don't need one".
- **Option 7 or 10** lead with the sustainability side. Stronger if the S-1 and subsidy segment turns out to be the heart of the conversation.
- **Option 9** is the closest to our proven "How to use" shape, but softens the take.
