# Title options — 2026-10-06

**LOCKED: `Grok Bot vs Dots vs Muse: Do You Even Need an AI Personal Assistant?`** Chosen by Vincent on 5 Oct. No `[LIVE]` prefix on this episode.

Scope: the always-on personal assistant wave (Grok Bot, dots, Muse) and whether it is sustainable, for the founder paying for one and for the lab selling it. Coding is the one job that fills 24 hours; take it out and most founders do not have enough for a 24/7 assistant. The EU is a talking point inside the show, not the headline.

## Why this title

- It names all three products people are searching for and spells out "personal assistant".
- The question is the one the episode answers, and none of the big dots or Muse videos asks it. Those videos cover what the agents can do, not whether you have enough work for one.
- "X vs Y" head-to-head titles are working this week. Nate Herk's dots vs Muse test has 354K views (yt-dlp scrape, 5 Oct).

## The other four considered

- `Grok Bot, Dots, Muse: Is a 24/7 AI Personal Assistant Worth Paying For?` (the money angle)
- `Grok Bot vs Dots vs Muse: Are You Busy Enough for an AI Personal Assistant?`
- `Grok Bot vs Dots vs Muse: Your AI Personal Assistant Has Nothing To Do` (strongest take; commits to the conclusion up front)
- `Grok Bot, Dots or Muse: Which AI Personal Assistant Should Founders Use?` (closest to the "how to use" shape, softest)

Not used: anything like "We Tested Dots and Muse". Neither is available to us in the EU, so it would be false.
