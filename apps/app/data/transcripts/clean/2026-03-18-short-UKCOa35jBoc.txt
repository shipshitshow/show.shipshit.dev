Every founder building an AI tool is
asking the same question. What happened
when the next version of Claude makes my
product a feature? They are right to
worry. If you sell the tool, you are
racing against the model, but if you
sell the work, again, the output, every
improvement in the model only makes your
service faster, cheaper, harder to
compete with. A company might spend 10K
a year on QuickBooks and 100K on an
accountant to close the books, which is
like, yeah, nuts.
