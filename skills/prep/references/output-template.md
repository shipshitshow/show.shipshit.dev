# Source board template

Preserve supplied topic frontmatter. Fill only sections useful to this episode; rough timings are flexible cues. One title, one promise: at most three segments plus a close, and anything off-title goes to the after-show block.

Add `announcement_tweet:` to the frontmatter: one post announcing the live (the take, when, the stream link), under 280 characters. The app shows it on the episode's Posts page.

## Cold Open — "Viewer promise"

The only prepared line. Stream and audio checks happen before going live; a countdown or holding card, if any, comes before this.

- **First sentence (approve one):** the claim plus the stake, under 15 seconds, in the hosts' words. Two alternates below it, each with the receipt it rests on. Mark an unchecked fact `CHECK BEFORE SAYING`.
- On screen with both hosts: the source passage, screenshot or ready result.
- By about 45 seconds: the three questions guiding the episode and the first useful point.
- Budget: about 1 minute. Exit: into segment one.

See [the opening guide](opening-and-hooks.md).

## Sources — Viewer decision

### Question and opening cue

What part of the title's promise does this segment answer? What preliminary answer can the hosts support?

### Story and stance

- **Vincent:** firsthand story and one number, in his words, or `No firsthand: ask`. Verdict in one line, or `Open`.
- **Mitchell:** the same for him.
- **Stance:** `Split`, `Agree` or `Open`. Record only what each host says. Where `Agree`, write the sceptical customer's question and who asks it.

### Evidence to open

- Source line both hosts have read: [useful passage](https://example.com) — event date, exact fact, uncertainty.
- X discussion: direct tweet link — question or disagreement, not a substitute for proof.
- Firsthand example: actual result and what was not checked.

### Talking points

- The claim or question, in under 12 words.
- **Challenge:** the opposing take, in a few words.
- **So what:** the viewer's choice.

Hosts glance at these during the live, so 2–5 bullets, one short line each: no full sentences, no explanations, no instructions to the hosts. Put the detail in Evidence to open.

### Producer notes

- Rough budget and exit cue, what to open on screen, what to avoid. The app hides this subsection from the talking points.
- **Re-hook:** one sentence at the planned elapsed time (every 8–10 minutes): what is answered, what is still coming.
- **Chat question:** the one to read aloud for this segment, planted if needed.

## Sources — Demo

Fixture, quota/readiness, expected proof, short recovery path and recorded/local fallback. What each host says while it runs, or run the wait off air. Omit an unavailable demo promise.

## Sources — Close and parking lot

### Verdicts

Answer the title's question using only what the show established. One line per host: what they would do on Monday. State what remains unknown. The show ends here.

### After the show

Up to five minutes, after the verdicts: catch-up, housekeeping and off-title items. For each parked item, say whether it goes here, to the next episode or is dropped. Park tangents here rather than silently growing the show.

For multiple app topic files, keep the full run of show in a separate note and each cluster in its own topic file. Use `###` inside a section so `##` remains an app section boundary. Attach [the on-air checklist](on-air-checklist.md) to the run sheet.
