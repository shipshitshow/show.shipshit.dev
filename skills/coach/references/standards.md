# On-air standards the coach scores against

These are the show's own rules, taken from its editorial direction and the 10 Nov
2026 quality audit. Change them at their source, then here. A rule here is a
prompt for the coach to look, not a target to hit.

| Standard | What the coach looks for |
|---|---|
| Open with the payoff (editorial format, 8 Oct 2026) | In the first 15 s after going live: the question and why a founder cares. By about 45 s: the first useful point. Greetings, chat and housekeeping come after the first takeaway. |
| Start on the claim (audit, 10 Nov) | The first sentence on air is the episode's promise. Catch-up and "how was your week" are late or cut. |
| Take sides (audit) | Each host has a view per segment. A reaction that asks what it cost, what broke or whether they would ship it counts more than "yeah". Nothing is faked: no manufactured disagreement. |
| Story first, source second (audit) | Each segment opens with something that happened to a host, with a number. The source is one line both have read. |
| Never read cold (audit) | "What is that? I don't know" about something on screen means it should not have been in the show. |
| Fill or cut the silence (audit) | Long waits are narrated or prepared off air. |
| Close on a verdict (audit) | Each host says in a sentence what they would do on Monday, then stops. |
| Use the chat (audit) | At least one viewer question per segment, even a planted one. |
| Drop the shrug words (audit) | "Whatever", "I don't know", "blah blah blah": pause instead. |
| Voice (product marketing context) | "I" for a host's own claim, "we" for shared show actions. Swearing occasional. |
| Call to action | Brief, at the end only, value-first. Never begging. |

Not scored from a transcript: eyes on the lens, framing, light, sound, screen-share
layout. The 30-second countdown before going live is deliberate and is not dead
air; times are measured from going live.

Speech numbers in the audit and here come from auto-captions with speaker-change
marks. They show direction, not exact counts.
