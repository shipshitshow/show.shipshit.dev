# Review shape

Delete template instructions from the output.

```markdown
# Growth review — YYYY-MM-DD

Source: inventory fetched YYYY-MM-DD; previous snapshot YYYY-MM-DD (or none);
retention file (or none). Missing: click-through, impressions (Studio export).

## Verdict
One line per episode: title, views, x median (n peers), band, and whether it is
old enough to judge.

| Published | Title | Age (d) | Views | Peer median | x median | Rank | Gained since snapshot |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Where viewers left
The mark with the largest gap against peers, the moment in the transcript, and
what is and is not known about why.

## Levers (last 3-4 episodes only)
| Lever | Episodes | Ratios | Keep / change / drop | Evidence limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Pull next
Retention for these episodes; click-through for these videos.

## Unlist candidates (proposal only)
Title, views, age. Waiting for a named go-ahead.
```
