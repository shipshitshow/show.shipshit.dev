## Visual direction

Canonical source: [the vault brand kit](https://github.com/shipshitshow/vault/tree/master/brand), including [identity](https://github.com/shipshitshow/vault/blob/master/brand/identity.md), [application rules](https://github.com/shipshitshow/vault/blob/master/brand/applications.md), [editing rules](https://github.com/shipshitshow/vault/blob/master/brand/editing.md), [tokens](https://github.com/shipshitshow/vault/blob/master/brand/tokens.json) and [asset inventory](https://github.com/shipshitshow/vault/blob/master/brand/inventory.json). Read the relevant guide and actual asset status before creating show artwork. This summary is not an independent palette or asset source; when the kit changes, update this canonical profile and run the existing profile sync.

Compact mark: **`SH*W`**, capital S/H/W with an asterisk replacing O, all four glyphs on one horizontal line. Full name remains **Ship Sh!t Show**. Keep letters broad/readable with a clear gap around the asterisk. Requested avatar direction: black mark on edge-to-edge red square, without border. Black/white form the foundation; red is a restrained identifying accent. Do not revive earlier `SH!P`/`SH!T`, stacked/compressed lettering, braces, rockets or cube concepts. A newer explicit user correction overrides defaults.

Use host faces and the full name in covers/banners; episode thumbnails combine a source-grounded idea, relevant actual photography and a small consistent signature. Broadcast graphics are modest: small corner mark, compact names/topic label, readable demos and a narrow optional company logo/URL strip. Natural-color footage is the default; editorial stills may use a coherent monochrome treatment. Keep captions and final typography editable, timed from the actual edit.

The inventory distinguishes `working-export`, `draft-reference` and `approved-export`. Generated boards contain illustrative poses/scenes and sample text; they are not real episode evidence, final overlays, exact font/color masters or publishable host-reference media. Do not crop board logos into masters. The public kit includes guides and the standalone logo only. All four face-containing concept boards and original portraits remain private, outside public repositories; obtain separately authorized media from the owner. Record kit revision, selected logo hash, fonts and approved deviations in editing handoff.

Use supplied original host photos and brand references. Preserve likeness. Do not assume local Desktop paths exist on a viewer's machine. Keep livestream and recap packaging distinct: two hosts may suit a live discussion; one optional host and readable brief text may suit a recap. Hosts, text and episode numbers are creative choices, not universal requirements. Surgical corrections preserve all unrequested details.

Channel branding and broadcast assets should feel related without reproducing the same busy composition in every scene. A PFP needs recognition at a tiny circular crop; a banner needs responsive safe-area composition; a screen demo needs room for readable product content.
