# Cue-sheet shape

Use only fields supported by the episode. Delete template instructions from output.

```markdown
# Live cues — YYYY-MM-DD — Episode title

Source notes: [topic](topic-NN-slug.md)
Planned total: N minutes. All schedule times are elapsed from stream start.
Live position: not reported. Actual coverage: not reported.
Last presented card: none.
Stream URL / wall-clock start: unconfirmed (omit when not useful).

Viewer question: the one question this episode answers.

On-air checklist: `skills/prep/references/on-air-checklist.md` — read before going live.

## 0:00 — Opening (after the countdown)

**First sentence (approved):** the board's claim plus stake, under 15 seconds, said to the lens.
**On screen with both hosts:** source passage, screenshot or ready result.
**By about 0:45:** the three questions guiding the episode and the first useful point.
**Not here:** stream checks, greetings, catch-up, a like or subscribe ask.

| Planned elapsed | Segment | First link | Main cue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–1:00 | Opening | none | First sentence, three questions |
| start–end | First source cluster | [source](URL) | Question the segment answers |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

## start–end — Segment name

**Story:** each host's story and number as the board records it, or `No firsthand: ask`.
**Stance:** Split / Agree / Open (from the hosts' own one-line verdicts).
**Open:** [source number / descriptive label](URL) — passage or result to show.
**Say:** Short prompt in the host's own voice; distinguish experience from evidence.
**Ask:** One co-host question; where hosts agree, the sceptical customer's question.
**Chat:** The one chat question to read aloud for this segment.
**Re-hook:** At the planned elapsed time: what is answered, what is still coming.
**Move on:** Transition to the next question, or wrap up/end for the close card.
**Hot take #N:** Optional mapped cue with its receipt and risk, in the host's words.
**Short S#:** Working title — self-contained entry cue; answer/caveat to land before the exit.
**Watch:** Material caveat, counter-evidence or hot-take risk, if needed.

Repeat a compact card for each scheduled segment. The final card is the close:
the title's answer, one Monday-verdict per host, what remains unknown, then the
after-show block (catch-up, housekeeping, parked items), five minutes at most.
Keep extra source links directly below their card as backups.
Allocate unbudgeted minutes to a named close and buffer so the total is explicit.

## Editor handoff — planned Shorts

| Candidate | Parent segment | Coverage / actual markers | Proof and caveat | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 — Working title | Source segment | Not reported; planned times are not clip timestamps | Evidence to show and qualification to keep | Planned |

Include 3–5 mapped candidates for new episode preparation, with at least one
controversial angle backed by evidence and a competing interpretation. After
recording, use captured / needs context / drop; verify exact start/end against
video/VTT. A cue being presented does not confirm capture.

## Cuts and parking lot

First cut: the episode's explicit priority, if supplied.
Further cuts: optional tangents, backup discussion, then compressed core segments.
Demo fallback: conditional slot and a usable discussion alternative.
Optional: tangents outside the timed plan.
```

For an on-air response, show only the requested card and its next segment. When
replanning, label remaining time as relative to the host's reported position; keep
the original plan and any confirmed coverage distinguishable.
