# Approved Thumbnail Lab winners (6 November 2026)

The owner reviewed every channel thumbnail in the Thumbnail Lab on 6 November 2026 and approved 67 current versions (64 landscape, 3 Shorts covers). Four were still marked for revision. Use these as reference patterns for new art. They are approved concepts, not measured performance winners. The approved images stay in the private Lab data, not in this repository.

## What the owner's review notes keep asking for

- **Readable at a glance.** Rejected: "confusing AF", "completely out of topic. can't understand it", "I dont understand the idea of this thumbnail." A viewer must guess the title from the image alone. One simple object beats a clever scene.
- **The right logo, large and recognisable, and only logos that belong.** Rejected: "can't even recognise NPM logo", "Fable 5 logo is not visible". A Codex-built project gets Altman, not Amodei. When a person carries the topic, the person is enough: "only Dario is enough", "no brand logo".
- **Not generic.** Rejected: "too classic. boring." The edited video and the livestream on the same topic need different designs ("this is the same as the livestream one").
- **Big faces with emotion.** A real person's face large enough to read at small size: panic, a stop gesture at the lens, looking at the viewer, or a face-off.
- **Show the product or the idea literally.** The content studio must look like a studio, the Pocket card must be the real card, a pricing video gets a big FREE tag.
- **Keep the likeness.** Rejected: "doesnt look like Theo. his likeliness is lost." Pass a reference photo and say to keep the face faithful.
- **One prop word is the only text exception.** A single word the owner asked for, printed on an object in the scene (FREE on a price tag, FIRED on a slip), or a logo's own lettering. Nothing else.

## How the winning prompts are written

- Attach the exact logo file and say "copied exactly", "never mirrored". Describe the mark in words too (for example the Anthropic A with its separate diagonal bar). Tell the model to ignore the logo file's background.
- Name the one hero and its size: "the biggest thing in the frame", "his face must be big and instantly recognizable at small size".
- When iterating, attach the current thumbnail and say what to keep and the one thing to change.
- Say "leave the top-right corner empty": the asterisk is stamped afterwards with `bun run thumbnail:stamp`, never generated.
- State the text rule outright: "no text", or "FREE is the only text allowed, spelled exactly F R E E".
